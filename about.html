About us

A phone store built like a spec sheet.

OmniMobilePH is a fictional multi-brand retailer created to solve a very real annoyance: hunting through scattered posts for a price, a specification, or a warranty answer.

The store

21 brands in one room, sorted the way buyers actually shop.

OmniMobilePH brings officially distributed brands and enthusiast import brands onto the same shelf. Instead of scrolling brand by brand, customers start with a budget tier, then compare devices across Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, Google, iQOO, REDMAGIC, and 13 more.

Every model has a published peso price, a tier label, and a reservation path. From the iPhone 18 Pro Max to a P4,299 Infinix Smart 9, the same rules apply.

The concept store sits on the third floor of Cyberzone, SM City North EDSA, with the online catalog mirroring the floor stock.
